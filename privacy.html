Privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

This policy covers these games, published on Google Play by Sivi Games:

  • Block Calm
  • Mahjong Zen Garden
  • Tile Trek
  • Sudoku Ladder

We do not collect anything. The games run entirely on your device, have no accounts or sign-in, and make no network connections.

What is stored, and where

Your progress, coins, settings and daily streak are saved in the game's own private storage on your device. Only that game can read them. Deleting the game deletes them, and there is no copy anywhere else. That also means we cannot restore your progress if you lose your device, and nobody can ask us for your information, because we do not hold any.

Each game also keeps a small diary of its own events, such as "level started", in the same private storage, to help trace a bug. It is never sent anywhere, it holds nothing about you, and it is deleted with the game.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or files.
  • We use no analytics service and no tracking of any kind.
  • We show no advertising and sell nothing inside the games.
  • We do not sell or share information about you, because none of it reaches us.

Permissions

The games ask for no special permissions and do not request the advertising identifier.

Children

The games collect nothing from anyone, children included.

Google Play

Google Play handles downloads and updates under Google's own privacy policy. We receive only the summary statistics Google gives every developer, such as install counts and crash reports that you choose to send to Google, and none of it identifies you.

Changes

If a future version adds advertising, purchases, or anything else that collects information, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the store listing will say what changed.
